Delete your NaamJaap account

App: NaamJaap (Jaap Bank)  |  Developer: KP Verse

You can delete your NaamJaap account and your data at any time. Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

अपना NaamJaap अकाउंट और डेटा आप कभी भी हटा सकते हैं। हटाने के बाद डेटा वापस नहीं आएगा।

Option 1: Delete inside the app (fastest)

  1. Open the NaamJaap app and log in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm when asked.

Your account is deleted right away.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot log in or the app is not installed, send an email:

To: kpboss.channal@gmail.com
Subject: Delete my NaamJaap account
Send it from: the same email address you used to sign up for NaamJaap.

We will delete your account and data within 15 days of receiving your request, and confirm by email.

What gets deleted

  • Your login (email and password)
  • Your public name
  • Your jaap deposits, balance, and statement history
  • Your Arpan records
  • Your private Mera Sankalp text and your app settings

What we keep

Nothing about you is kept after deletion, except data that the law requires us to retain, if any. Ordinary backups are removed in the normal course of Firebase operations.
