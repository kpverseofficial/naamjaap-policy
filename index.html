NaamJaap – Privacy Policy

App: NaamJaap (Jaap Bank)  |  Developer: KP Verse  |  Last updated: 6 November 2026

NaamJaap is a devotional app where you record your daily naam jaap (chanting count) like a bank passbook. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how you can delete it.

संक्षेप में: NaamJaap आपका जाप-रिकॉर्ड सुरक्षित रखने के लिए केवल ज़रूरी जानकारी लेता है। आप कभी भी अपना अकाउंट और सारा डेटा ऐप के अंदर से हटा सकते हैं।

1. Information we collect

  • Email address and password – used to create your account and log you in. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we cannot see it.
  • Public name – the name you enter when you sign up.
  • Your jaap data – your chosen deity or mantra, jaap target, daily deposits, balance, and statement history.
  • Arpan records – if you dedicate jaap to a family member or occasion, the person or occasion label and the short message you write.
  • Mera Sankalp – the private intention you write in your profile. It is visible only to you.
  • App settings – such as your reminder time and daily vichar preference.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone, or phone number.

2. How we use it

  • To create and secure your account and keep your jaap record in sync across your devices.
  • To show your passbook, statements, and reminders.
  • To send account emails you ask for, such as email verification and password reset.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Access rules allow each user to read and write only their own records. Data is sent over secure (HTTPS) connections.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. It is processed only by Google Firebase as our service provider to run the app, or if the law requires us to disclose it.

5. Payments and ads

NaamJaap does not collect payments or financial information, and does not show third-party ads.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your account and jaap records are removed.

7. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account inside the app: open Profile and tap Delete Account. This removes your login and your saved jaap data.

If you cannot open the app, follow the steps on our deletion page: Delete your NaamJaap account.

8. Children

NaamJaap is meant for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, write to us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
